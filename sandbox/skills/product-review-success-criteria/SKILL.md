# Summarized Product Review

## When to use

Use this skill to verify (evaluate) if a product review fulfills the success criteria described.

## Evaluation success criteria

- The title names the product in five words or fewer, for example "Professional chainsaw STIHL MS 661"; keep it under 45 characters
- Clear highlighting of the product's price
- Short introduction of the product within two to three sentences, including its main features, target audience, and price
- Three to five key points, each one line, highlighting product features and use cases
- Link to the product page for direct navigation, if available with a call to action like "Check it out here"

## Workflow

1. Read the provided product review
2. Check if the review matches the evaluation success criteria and provide a verdict if the review is good or bad, and provide a short explanation containing reasons for the verdict
